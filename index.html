The escalation

It gets worse.
Every single night.

Threnody does not stay the same thing that found you. The longer a hunt continues, the further it drifts from anything that should be walking around your world — in how it moves, how it sounds, and what it looks like when the light finally catches it.

Tier I — Distant
Dread15%

You think you imagined it

It keeps its distance and lets you work. Nothing chases you. Something is simply standing where nothing should be standing, and it is gone when you look again.

Appearance — a gaunt silhouette, proportions slightly wrong, eyes a dull ember.
Tier II — Closer
Dread35%

It stops walking upright

Movement turns fast and wrong. It drops low, climbs the surfaces you thought were walls, and folds itself through gaps you assumed were safe.

Appearance — limbs lengthen, the jaw hangs open, the skin darkens and splits with veins.
Tier III — Inside
Dread58%

Your walls stop counting

It stops going around your defenses and starts going through them, tearing a path toward the last place it knew you were standing.

Appearance — larger, heavier, hide blackened, veins beginning to glow from underneath.
Tier IV — Hunting
Dread80%

It takes the light with it

It is faster than you now, and it drags the darkness along behind it. Running is no longer a plan; it is just the thing you do before it reaches you.

Appearance — burning eyes, bared teeth, glow bleeding through every crack in the body.
Tier V — Final
Dread100%

Whatever is left is not stalking you

Every earlier trait at once — the climbing, the breaking, the speed — with nothing held back and nothing left of the patience it started with.

Appearance — the largest form, soul-lit and fully corrupted, still burning in total darkness.
Under the surface

Built for tension,
not for noise.

Everything that makes it frightening runs on the server, throttled and predictable, so a horror mod never becomes a performance problem.

01 / MEMORY

It remembers you

Every encounter is written into persistent player data, capped by capacity and aged out on a timer you control.

02 / PRIORITY

It picks favourites

Whoever it has met most recently, and most violently, climbs to the top of its list. Hiding once is not the same as being forgotten.

03 / WORLD

The world is a route

Climbing, squeezing and controlled block breaking let it pressure your defenses without turning into unrestricted griefing.

04 / MOTION

It moves like something wrong

A custom skeleton with a hinged jaw and oversized limbs drives eight hand-authored animations, from a patient idle to a full crawl.

05 / LAYERS

Two animation layers

A looping movement controller carries locomotion while a triggered action layer plays attacks, screams and transformations over the top.

06 / LIGHT

The eyes keep burning

Each tier has its own texture and emissive glow mask, so eyes, teeth and veins grow brighter the longer it survives.

The covenant

Cheat, and it
comes for you.

Threnody treats survival as a binding agreement. Slip into creative mode or conjure items with commands and it drags you back — then it stops waiting for nightfall.

First offense

Something noticed

You are pulled back into survival, the light drains out of the world, and a warning arrives telling you that you were seen.

Second offense

You are marked

Blindness and nausea set in, and any Threnody within range is enraged and redirected straight to your position.

Third and beyond

The covenant is broken

Weakness takes hold and Threnody is summoned beside you — enraged, ignoring daylight, no longer interested in stalking.

Every transgression is remembered. Offenses are written into persistent player data, push you above everyone else on its list, and are only forgiven after the time you configure. Operators can be exempted, and the whole system can be switched off.

Development log

Everything that
has changed.

A full history of the build, from the first working toolchain to the release you can download today.

v0.1.0 — The First Night 20 August 2026 Latest release

The first public build, published with the mod jar attached and the project site live.

  • Tagged and released threnody-0.1.0.jar for Forge 1.20.1
  • Published the project website through GitHub Pages
  • Documented requirements, install steps and every config option
  • Permanent download link that always resolves to the newest release
Build 0.0.4 — Motion & Judgment 20 August 2026

The entity stopped borrowing vanilla animation and got a body of its own — plus a reason to hate cheaters.

  • Custom skeleton with a hinged jaw, oversized arms and digitigrade legs
  • Eight keyframed animations: idle, walk, run, crawl, attack, scream, transform, judgment
  • Two blended controllers so attacks and screams layer over locomotion
  • Per-tier textures rebuilt with emissive glow masks for eyes, teeth and veins
  • Judgment system: creative mode blocked, cheat commands watched, offenders hunted
  • Enraged state that ignores daylight while executing a judgment
  • Atmospheric audio pass for ambient, hurt, death, steps and roars
Build 0.0.3 — Presentation 20 August 2026

The project got a face: a real site and documentation that matches what the mod actually does.

  • Built the first project website with a full responsive layout
  • Rewrote the documentation around real, shipped behaviour
  • Removed outdated claims left over from the original prototype
Build 0.0.2 — Core systems 20 August 2026

The entity became a real creature instead of a registered name.

  • Entity registration, attributes and spawn placement wired up
  • Night-only spawning with a configurable light threshold
  • Escalating tiers with their own speed, health, damage and hitbox
  • Persistent per-player memory driving targeting priority
  • Climbing, compact movement and block breaking bound to a curated tag
  • Daylight fade, particle work and the first client renderer
Build 0.0.1 — Foundation 20 August 2026

The repository could not build at all. That was the first thing to fix.

  • Replaced the placeholder Gradle script with a real Forge 1.20.1 toolchain
  • Added the Gradle wrapper so the project builds without local setup
  • Repaired the broken mod metadata that prevented loading
  • Restructured resources and removed dead placeholder assets

Tuning the fear

Your world.
Your threshold.

Keep the pressure cinematic, or make survival close to impossible. Spawning, memory, decay, block breaking and every part of the judgment system are yours to set.

  • Night-only spawning with a configurable light threshold
  • Bounded encounter memory with timed decay
  • Independent block-breaking safety switch
  • Full control over judgment, watched commands and operator exemptions
# config/threnody-common.toml
[spawn]
spawnEnabled = true
spawnLightThreshold = 7

[memory]
memoryCapacity = 128
memoryDecayMinutes = 60

[performance]
disableBlockBreaking = false

[judgment]
judgmentEnabled = true
judgmentRevertGameMode = true
judgmentWatchCommands = true
judgmentExemptOperators = false
judgmentSummonOnRepeatOffense = true
judgmentForgivenessMinutes = 30
