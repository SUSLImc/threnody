The progression

Every night makes
it worse.

Threnody is not a collection of skins. Each stage changes the threat profile, movement, attack strength, vulnerabilities, and the way it occupies the world.

STAGE 01

Stalker

The base form. Quiet, patient, and fast enough to punish an exposed player.

STAGE 02

Crawler

Climbs when blocked and turns close-quarters encounters into a slowing attack.

STAGE 03

Squeeze

A compact collision profile lets it slip through one-block-high openings.

STAGE 04

Breaker

Respects mob griefing and a curated block tag while dismantling the route ahead.

STAGE 05

Huntmaster

Stronger, quicker, and able to push darkness into the player's field of view.

STAGE 06

Eschaton

The final escalation: climbing, breaking, pressure, and a soul-lit warning.

Under the surface

Built for tension,
not noise.

Designed to keep multiplayer safe and performance predictable while making the threat feel personal.

01 / MEMORY

It remembers you

Encounter history is stored per player in persistent NBT, bounded by capacity, and aged out through configurable decay.

02 / WORLD

The world is a route

Climbing, squeezing, and controlled block breaking let the entity pressure familiar defenses without unrestricted griefing.

03 / SERVER

The server stays in charge

Target selection, stage changes, effects, and environmental interactions are authoritative and throttled for multiplayer.

04 / MOTION

It moves like something wrong

A custom skeleton with a hinged jaw and oversized limbs drives eight hand-authored GeckoLib animations, from a patient idle to a full crawl.

05 / BLENDING

Two animation layers

A looping movement controller carries locomotion while a triggered action controller plays attacks, screams, and transformations over the top.

06 / LIGHT

The eyes keep burning

Every stage has its own texture and emissive glow mask, so eyes, teeth, and veins grow brighter as the entity evolves.

The covenant

Cheat, and it
comes for you.

Threnody treats survival as a binding agreement. Slip into creative mode or conjure items with commands and it drags you back — then it stops waiting for nightfall.

First offense

Something noticed

You are pulled back into survival, the light drains out of the world, and a warning arrives that you were seen.

Second offense

You are marked

Blindness and nausea set in, and any Threnody within range is enraged and redirected straight to your position.

Third and beyond

The covenant is broken

Weakness takes hold and Threnody is summoned beside you — enraged, ignoring daylight, and no longer interested in stalking.

Every transgression is remembered. Offenses are written into persistent player data, raise your targeting priority above everyone else's, and are only forgiven after the configured time. Operators can be exempted, and the entire system can be switched off.

Tuning the fear

Your world.
Your threshold.

Keep the pressure cinematic or make survival nearly impossible. The common config controls spawning, memory, decay, and block-breaking behavior.

  • Night-only spawning with a configurable light threshold
  • Bounded encounter memory per player
  • Configurable memory decay in minutes
  • Independent block-breaking safety switch
  • Full control over judgment, watched commands, and operator exemptions
# config/threnody-common.toml
[spawn]
spawnEnabled = true
spawnLightThreshold = 7

[memory]
memoryCapacity = 128
memoryDecayMinutes = 60

[performance]
disableBlockBreaking = false

[judgment]
judgmentEnabled = true
judgmentRevertGameMode = true
judgmentWatchCommands = true
judgmentExemptOperators = false
judgmentSummonOnRepeatOffense = true
judgmentForgivenessMinutes = 30
